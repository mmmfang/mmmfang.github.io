Sew What's Next — AI-powered sewing project planner

Built for sewists, home sewers, and sewing hobbyists who want to get more from their fabric stash. Sew What's Next answers the question every home sewer faces — what should I make with what I already own? Browse your stash, match patterns to fabric automatically, and get AI-generated styling ideas, all in one place. No download required.

  • Pattern-to-fabric matching engine — automatically pairs patterns to your stash based on fabric type, yardage, and stretch requirements
  • AI styling inspiration — generates curated project ideas per pattern with Pinterest search integration

  • Full fabric stash management — photos, yardage, cost tracking, CSV bulk import, and stash value totals
  • Create a free account to save your stash and patterns, or explore the full experience instantly with demo mode — no sign-up needed
  • Mobile-first design with dark and light mode — works on any device, any screen
